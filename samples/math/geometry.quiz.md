# problem:multiple_choice

## question

The angles in a triangle are $45^\circ$ and $65^\circ$. What is the third angle?

## option

$60^\circ$

## option

$80^\circ$

## option:correct

$70^\circ$

## explanation

The interior angles total $180^\circ$:

$$180 - 45 - 65 = 70$$

# problem:multiple_choice

## question

What is the area of a circle with radius $r$?

## option

$2\pi r$

## option:correct

$\pi r^2$

## option

$\pi r$

## explanation

Area is $A = \pi r^2$; $2\pi r$ is the circumference.

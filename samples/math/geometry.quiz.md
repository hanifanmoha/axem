# problem:multiple_choice

## question

The angles in a triangle are $45^\circ$ and $65^\circ$. What is the third angle?

<details>
<summary>Hint</summary>

The three interior angles of any triangle always add up to the same total.

</details>

## option

$60^\circ$

## option

$80^\circ$

## option:correct

$70^\circ$

## explanation

The interior angles total $180^\circ$:

$$
180 - 45 - 65 = 70
$$

# problem:multiple_choice

## question

What is the area of a circle with radius $r$?

## option

$2\pi r$

## option:correct

$\pi r^2$

## option

$\pi r$

## explanation

Area is $A = \pi r^2$; $2\pi r$ is the circumference.

<details>
<summary>Why the two are easy to swap</summary>

Squaring the radius grows faster than doubling it. With $r = 3$:

- Area $= 9\pi \approx 28.27$
- Circumference $= 6\pi \approx 18.85$

So once $r > 2$ the area is always the larger number — a quick sanity check in an exam.

</details>
